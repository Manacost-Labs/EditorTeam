# better-writing

Production-grade English and Russian writing and revision skill for prose that must stay accurate, useful, traceable to its sources, consistent across a project, and recognisably owned by its writer.

It supports six jobs: drafting, rewriting, line editing, review, humanisation, and adaptation. The workflow protects facts and exact literals, fixes structure before style, restores voice without invented personality, and finishes with explicit fidelity and quality gates.

## What it adds

- a preservation contract for facts, uncertainty, quotations, commands, identifiers, citations, and voice anchors
- genre-specific shapes for developers, writers, product owners, operators, and customer-facing teams
- publication-informed, original cross-genre examples for natural structure and digestible long prose
- paragraph, sentence, evidence, voice, and humanisation passes with observable stop conditions
- worked technical, editorial, memo, product, email, UI, and reflective rewrites that remove formulae by rebuilding the thought
- relation-first em-dash, semicolon, and colon transformations with natural sentence-flow patterns
- a researched, searchable formulaic-language catalogue with remove, rewrite, review, and protect actions
- meaning-first rewrites across vague change frames, canned significance, benefit fog, generic authority, assistant residue, and formulaic openings or endings, without synonym swapping
- an extensible machine-readable corpus and optional Python scanner with literal, technical, quotation, and locality protections
- release-grade behavioural, routing, edge, disclosure, and near-miss evals
- minimal, standard, and deep intervention levels plus clean, annotated, review-only, and side-by-side output modes
- Russian grammar, typography, register, uncertainty, and humanisation guidance with dedicated fixtures and routing tests
- deterministic preservation checks for exact literals, numbers, quotations, code, URLs, paths, and English or Russian uncertainty markers
- an executable forward-eval harness that can run a model command or grade captured outputs against observable constraints
- a PDF and large-corpus workflow for born-digital files, scans, OCR, tables, page-level provenance, extraction-quality checks, and resumable source-backed synthesis
- a standard-library corpus tool that inventories files, hashes and deduplicates them, records PDF page counts when available, and produces bounded text chunks without pretending to OCR PDFs
- a reusable JSON project profile for audiences, deliverables, terminology, approved facts, voice, output, and privacy rules, with a dependency-free validator
- a portable Source Pack layout for raw files, extraction, chunks, manifests, issues, claim ledgers, and incremental deliverables
- a deterministic claim audit that rejects unsupported used claims, undisclosed conflicts, unresolved evidence, and unlabelled editorial synthesis

`SKILL.md` is the canonical instruction file. References provide branch-specific depth; scripts provide optional deterministic diagnostics and package validation.

The skill edits and synthesises natural-language prose. It preserves code, identifiers, selectors, schema keys, configuration keys, source locators, and other machine-readable constructs exactly, including when they appear inside documentation. Project profiles provide defaults but never override the current request, evidence, approvals, or safety boundaries. The AI-ism scanner remains English-specific; Russian editing uses the locale profile and behavioral evals instead. PDF extraction, OCR, rendering, statistical analysis, and charting remain delegated to specialised tools; Better Writing controls editorial structure, fidelity, provenance, and claim coverage.
